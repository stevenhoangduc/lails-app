1. What Lails is

Lails is a free mobile app for nail technicians and nail professionals. It has a feed, profiles, direct messages (Tám), a local marketplace (Chợ chồm hổm), a jobs board (Tìm ông bà nội), Shop Reviews, a rooms-for-rent board (Mướn phòng) and an optional singles section (Se duyên).

Lails is a place for members to meet and post. Lails is not part of any sale, rental, job or relationship between members. We don't check members, employers, landlords, sellers or singles.

Lails is free. We don't handle payments between members.

2. You must be 18 or older

You must be at least 18 years old to use Lails. By signing up you confirm that the date of birth you give is true. We ban accounts of people under 18 and delete them. Don't help anyone under 18 use Lails.

  • Keep your password safe. You are responsible for what happens in your account. Tell us at lailssupport@gmail.com if you think someone else used it.

Keep the authenticator app: if you lose it, you can only get back in, or delete your account, through Lails support, which checks that the request comes from the email address on your account before turning two-step login off.

  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile tab → Settings → Delete account). See the Privacy Policy and How to delete your Lails account for what is deleted.

6. Reporting, blocking, suspension and bans

  • Report: every post, comment, profile, listing, job ad, room ad, shop, shop review and Se duyên profile has a Report button. Reporters are never shown to anyone.
  • Block: you can block any member. You then don't see each other, and neither of you can send messages in your chat.
  • What we do: we aim to check reports at least once a day. We may dismiss a report, delete the content, remove a cover photo, ban the member from Chợ chồm hổm, ban the member from Se duyên, or ban the account.
  • Bans: a banned (suspended) account can only see a "Your account has been suspended" screen with our contact email, "Delete my account" and "Sign out". Its profile and content are hidden from everyone. We may lift a ban. While an account is banned, the same email, Google or Apple account can't sign up again, also after the banned account is deleted.

  • We may also suspend or end your access if we must by law, or to protect members or Lails.

7. Chợ chồm hổm (marketplace)

  • Any member can post a listing: new nail products, used nail products, homemade food, or other items ("Đồ khác", anything else that is allowed, for example a car or a phone).
  • Lails is not part of any sale. We don't take payment, deliver, track orders, check items or check sellers. Buyers and sellers agree on price, payment and pickup themselves, in Tám. Local pickup only.
  • You are responsible for your own deal and for following local laws, including food safety and food sale laws for homemade food, and taxes.
  • Banned items: weapons and ammunition; drugs and medicines (including prescription medicine); alcohol; tobacco and vapes; live animals; fake, counterfeit or stolen goods; adult content; anything illegal where you or the buyer live. We delete listings for banned items. The app shows this list when a member picks "Other items".
  • Don't put your exact address in a listing. Share pickup details only in private messages.
  • Scams: be careful. Meet in a public place, check the item before you pay, and never send money to someone you haven't met. Report scams.
  • No liability: Lails is not responsible for the items, their quality, safety or legality, for payments, or for any loss, injury or dispute from a sale. See sections 12 and 13.

8. Shop Reviews

  • Members can add nail shops and rate shops they worked at: pay, management, cleanliness, hours, tip policy and culture.
  • Reviews are shown without the author's name. Lails knows who wrote each review.
  • Write only about your own, real experience. Be honest and fair. Write facts and your opinion, not rumours.
  • No fake reviews: no reviews of a shop you didn't work at, no reviews for pay or a reward, no reviews of your own shop or a competitor, no more than one review per shop.
  • Don't name or identify people (coworkers, owners, customers), and don't share private information.
  • Shop owners: you can report a review as "false or harmful information" in the app, or, if you aren't a member, write to lailssupport@gmail.com with the shop, the review and why. We look at it and may remove it. Shop owners can't reply to reviews at launch.
  • We may remove any review or shop, merge duplicate shops, or ban a review's author.
  • Reviews are members' opinions, not Lails' opinions. Lails doesn't check them.

  • Lails is not part of any rental agreement. We are not a landlord, agent or broker. We don't check rooms, landlords or tenants, and we don't handle rent or deposits. You agree on price, rules and the rental yourselves.
  • You must follow housing laws, including fair housing laws. Don't state a preference or limit based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, family status or other protected traits, except where the law allows it for shared living.
  • Don't post an exact street address, and don't show house numbers or street signs in photos.
  • Scams: never pay a deposit or rent before you have seen the room and met the person. Report scams.
