How to delete your Lails account

Lails is a social app for nail technicians, made by Steve Hoang Nguyen. This page explains how to delete your Lails account and your data, what we delete, and what we keep.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lails and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab → Settings → Delete account. The screen lists what will be deleted.
  3. Confirm it's you. If you use an email and password, enter your password. If you use Google or Apple, you sign in with Google or Apple again. On an Android phone, after signing in with Google your phone also asks for its own lock (fingerprint, face unlock, or the phone PIN, pattern or password), if one is set.
  4. Tap Delete my account permanently.
  5. If you turned on two-step login, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and tap Continue.

Your account is deleted straight away and this can't be undone. You are logged out, and to use Lails again you would need to sign up as a new member. "Delete account" is also on the sign-up screens, so you can leave before you finish signing up. A suspended (banned) member can still delete their account from the suspension screen.

Lost your authenticator app? Email us (see below) and we will reset your two-step login first, or delete the account for you.

Can't open the app?

Email lailssupport@gmail.com from the email address you used for Lails, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and account details: email, password, date of birth, area and city, profile picture and its stored file
  • Your posts, with all their photos and videos
  • Your likes and comments, and the likes and comments on your posts
  • The members you follow, your followers and the members you blocked
  • Every Tám message you sent
  • Your Chợ chồm hổm listings, Mướn phòng room ads and Tìm ông bà nội job ads, with their photos
  • The shop reviews you wrote
  • Your Se duyên profile and photos, if you joined
  • The reports you made
  • Your notifications, notification settings and the notification tokens for your phones
  • Your two-step login authenticators and your search limit counter
  • Your login on your phone

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies of deleted data may stay in our database backups for up to 7 days.
  • Request logs: our hosting provider's request logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for 7 days.
  • Login records: our login provider's audit log keeps your email address and IP address for sign-ups and logins. It is not deleted when you delete your account today, and has no end date yet.
  • Tám chats: the person you chatted with keeps the messages they sent you. A chat is deleted once both people have deleted their accounts.
  • Shops you added to Shop Reviews stay, without your name.
  • Banned accounts: if the account was banned, a scrambled code (a keyed one-way hash) of its email and Google or Apple account is kept with no end date, so the same email or account can't sign up again. It has no link to any other data.
  • Owner records: if the owner reset your two-step login or deleted your account for you, a record of it is kept with no end date: the account id, which owner, when, and (for a reset) how many authenticators and logins were removed. No email, name or reason.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete these yourself in the app, at any time:

  • A post, with all its photos and videos
  • Your comments, and any comment on your own posts
  • Your likes (tap the like again) and the members you follow (Unfollow)
  • Your Chợ chồm hổm listings, Mướn phòng room ads and Tìm ông bà nội job ads; you can also remove photos from a listing or room ad when you edit it
  • Your shop reviews
  • Your Se duyên photos (at least 3 must stay while you are in Se duyên), or Leave Se duyên, which deletes your Se duyên profile ("About me", "I am" and "Show me"), all your Se duyên photos and your agreement to the Se duyên text
  • Your two-step login key, by turning two-step login off

Your profile picture can be replaced at any time but not removed, because every profile has one. Single Tám messages can't be deleted in the app. For these, or anything else, email lailssupport@gmail.com from the email address you used for Lails and tell us what you want deleted.
