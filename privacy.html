Lails Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated 10 November 2026, pending legal review. This version applies to the Lails test app. It will be replaced by the final version before Lails launches.

Effective date: Set at launch

Who we are: Lails is run by Steven Nguyen, California, USA ("Lails", "we", "us").

Contact: lailssupport@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • Lails is a social app for nail technicians. You must be 18 or older.
  • We collect what the app needs to work: your email, date of birth, one profile picture, your approximate area and city, and what you post and send (and, if you choose, a cover photo).
  • We never collect your precise GPS location or your street address.
  • If you don't share your phone location, we use your internet (IP) address to guess your city, and our code doesn't save it.
  • We don't sell your data, and the app has no ads and no analytics.
  • You can delete your account and your data in the app, at any time.
  • Other members can find you by name in Search. You can switch this off ("Show me in search").

2. Who can use Lails

Lails is only for people who are 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up, also if you use Google or Apple, and we refuse anyone under 18, in the app and on our servers. The date of birth is what you tell us. We don't check ID. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we ban it and delete it. If you think a member is under 18, report their profile ("under 18" is a report reason).

Testers before launch. Before Lails opens to everyone, invited testers use a test version on our test project (also in the United States, at Supabase). This applies to the Android test on Google Play and to iPhone testers (TestFlight). Testers sign up with Google (or Apple on an iPhone), and this policy applies to them. Our team's automated tests also run on the test project. Test accounts and everything posted during the test are deleted before launch.

3. What we collect, and why

3.1 Your account

  • Email address and password. To create your account, verify it, log you in and reset your password. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of your password.
  • Google or Apple sign-in. If you use them, Google or Apple tells us an account id and your email address. Apple may give a private relay email instead. We ask Apple for your email only, not your name. Google normally also shares your name and profile picture link, and our login provider saves what Google sends. We don't use your Google name or picture in Lails.
  • Date of birth. To check you are 18 or older. It is never shown to other members. It can't be changed after sign-up. Se duyên shows your age in whole years, worked out from your date of birth, never the date itself.
  • Your phone's time zone name (for example "Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh"), so the 18+ check uses today's date where you are. If you sign up with email, it is kept with your account details. If you sign up with Google or Apple, it is used for the check and not kept.
  • App language (English or Vietnamese), so the app and our emails are in your language. Other members see a small language label on your profile.
  • Notification token. If you allow notifications, we save a token for your phone (and whether it is an iPhone or an Android phone) so we can send them (at most the newest 10 phones per account), and your on/off choices in Settings (messages; likes, comments and new followers). Members can't see them. The token is deleted when you log out, when Expo tells us the app was removed from the phone, or when you delete your account. See 3.8.
  • Agreements. The version and time you agreed to the profile picture text; the version (with the language of the text you saw) and time you agreed to these terms and the Community rules (a required checkbox at sign-up; members who joined before it see a one-time screen; when we change them, you see a one-time "We have updated..." screen and agree again, and we keep only your newest agreement); and the time you tapped "I understand" on the Chợ chồm hổm, Mướn phòng and Tìm ông bà nội notices; and the version and time you agreed to the Se duyên text.

3.2 Your profile picture

To finish sign-up you add exactly one profile picture from your gallery. It must show you (a clear face photo or a full-body photo), so members know who they are talking to.

  • Members who finished sign-up can see it. People who aren't logged in can't. Members who can see it could save it or take a screenshot.
  • It is kept in private storage and shown through links that expire after a few minutes.
  • We remove location data (GPS) from the photo on your phone before it is uploaded.
  • We keep a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of the original photo file, only to stop the same photo being added twice. Members can't see it.
  • We don't use your picture for face recognition. We don't make or keep a face template, and we don't compare faces.
  • Face check on your phone. Before your profile picture (and each Se duyên photo) is uploaded, your phone checks that it shows one clear face (Google ML Kit, running on the phone with its model inside the app). A photo with no face, more than one face, or a face that is too small or unclear isn't uploaded. If the check can't run, the photo isn't uploaded either, and you can try again. The face details the check finds are used only for that yes or no answer: they are never stored, and never sent to us or anyone else. ML Kit itself may send Google usage diagnostics (for example device and app information, the event type, the image format and size, error codes and a per-installation id), never the image or the face results (Google's ML Kit data disclosure).
  • You can replace your picture at any time. You can't remove it without deleting your account, because every profile has one.

Cover photo (optional). You can also add one cover photo, shown in the banner at the top of your profile. It can be your nail work, your salon or another picture you have the right to use (Community rules); it doesn't have to show you, and there is no face check on it.

  • You pick it from your gallery. Your phone makes a smaller copy (longest side 1,600 pixels) without location data (GPS) or other photo details before it is uploaded.
  • Members who can see your profile can see your current cover (not members you blocked or who blocked you, and not banned members). They could save it or take a screenshot. It is kept in private storage and shown through links that expire after a few minutes.
  • You can change or remove it at any time; the app then deletes the old file in the background (best effort: if that fails, the file stays in your private folder, visible only to you, until you delete your account). Members can report a cover ("Cover photo breaks the community rules"). An owner can remove a reported cover: it then disappears from your profile, you can't use that same file as your cover again, and the file stays in your private folder, visible only to you, until you delete your account.

3.3 Face ID and fingerprint unlock (optional)

You can choose to unlock Lails with Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint. Your phone does the check. Lails only learns whether it passed. We never see or save your face or fingerprint. The on/off setting is saved on your phone only. On Android, the same kind of phone check is also asked before your account is deleted when you confirm with Google (see section 8); nothing is saved for it.

3.4 Your area and city

Lails shows how far away listings, ads and Se duyên singles are, without ever showing an address.

  • At sign-up we ask for your phone's approximate location only (never precise). If you say no, you type your city instead.
  • Your phone sends its approximate reading to our server. The server immediately rounds it to the center of a square on a fixed map grid (about 4.4 km wide) and finds the nearest city. We save only that square's center and the city name. We don't save the reading itself.
  • Other members see only your city and country and a rounded distance (for example "about 15 km" or "less than 5 km"). They never see coordinates.
  • Se duyên uses bigger squares (about 10 km) and coarser rounding.
  • Your listings, room ads and job ads use the same area by default. You can pick another city for each one.
  • You can change your area in Settings, at most 3 times a day. We keep the times of your changes for 24 hours to apply this limit.
  • We also record whether your area came from your phone, a typed city, or the IP guess below.

3.5 The one-time IP city guess

If you don't share your phone location, we pre-fill the city field with a guess from your internet (IP) address. You always confirm or change it.

  • The guess is made once each time you open the city field (at sign-up, or later in "Your area").
  • Our server reads the IP address of that one request, looks it up in a city table stored in our own database, and sends back only a suggested city.
  • Our code doesn't store or log the IP address, and it isn't sent to any other company for this.
  • Note: our hosting provider, Supabase, records IP addresses in its own request logs and login records, outside our code (see sections 6 and 7). On the planned Supabase Pro plan these logs are kept for 7 days.
  • The city and IP data come from free files by GeoNames and DB-IP, which we store ourselves. We send them nothing.

3.6 What you post and send

  • Profile details: display name, optional bio, specialties. Members can see them.
  • Posts: captions, photos and videos, with location data removed. Members can see them and could save them.
  • Likes, comments and follows. Members can see comments and like counts. Nobody can open a list of who liked a post. But the post's author is told: their Notifications show the name and picture of the newest member who liked the post and how many others did, and a push notification can say "Lan liked your post" (see 3.8). So an author who checks often can learn who liked their post. Follower and following counts show on profiles; only you see your own follows list. The member you follow is told in the same way.
  • Tám messages: private messages between two members. Only the two of you can read them in the app. They are not end-to-end encrypted. They are protected by access rules on our servers, and Lails can technically read them (for example to investigate a report). Our provider's live message service keeps a copy for up to 3 days.
  • Chợ chồm hổm listings (photos, title, description, price, category, city).
  • Tìm ông bà nội job ads (ad type, kinds of work, schedule, title, description, city, and an optional pay: one amount or a range, per hour, day, week or month or a commission percentage, and a currency). Your name and profile picture show on your ad, also on "looking for work" ads. Members who can see the ad see the pay.
  • Mướn phòng room ads (ad type, title, description, city, optional photos).
  • Shops you add to Shop Reviews (name, optional street name, city). Nobody is shown who added a shop.
  • Shop reviews (six ratings from 1 to 5, and optional text up to 1,000 characters). Reviews are shown without the author's name, dated only by month and year. A shop's ratings and reviews only show once it has at least 3. Lails itself can see who wrote a review, and the owner sees the author's name when a review is reported.
  • Saved items. When you tap the ❤️ on a Chợ chồm hổm listing, a Tìm ông bà nội job ad or a Mướn phòng room ad, we save which item you saved and when, for your "Đã lưu" (Saved) list. Only you can see it: the person who posted the item is never told and sees no count. A saved item that you can no longer see (for example after a block or when it expires) is kept but not shown. Your saves are deleted when you unsave them, when the item is deleted, or when you delete your account.
  • Se duyên. If you join (it's optional), we collect 3 to 6 photos of you, "I am" (woman, man or non-binary), "Show me" (women, men or everyone), an optional "About me", and an age range filter. See section 4.

3.7 Safety records

  • Reports: the reason and an optional note, on a post, comment, profile (including its cover photo), listing, job ad, room ad, shop, shop review or Se duyên profile. The reporter is never shown to anyone, also not to the owner.
  • Blocks: who you blocked, and their name as it was then (for your Blocked list). A block also removes your likes and comments on each other's posts, and unblocking doesn't bring them back.
  • Bans: the date, the owner who banned, and an optional private note. See section 7 for what we keep after a banned account is deleted.
  • Chợ chồm hổm ban: stops a member selling. Only owners see it. It lasts until lifted and is deleted with the account.
  • Cover photo removals: when an owner removes a reported cover photo, we record which file, whose it was and when (not which owner), so it can't be set again. Deleted with the account.
  • Se duyên ban: the member, the time and the owner who banned. Only owners see it. It lasts until lifted and is deleted with the account.
  • Word filter. Our servers refuse public text that contains a word or phrase on our banned list (ignoring capitals, extra spaces, look-alike letters and accents (except Vietnamese words whose accents change the meaning)): display names, bios, post captions, comments, the titles and descriptions of listings, room ads and job ads, shop names and streets, review text and the Se duyên "About me". Refused text isn't saved, and we never record which word matched. Tám messages and report notes are not checked.

3.8 Notifications (likes, comments and new followers)

  • What we save. When someone likes your post, comments on it or starts following you, we save a notification for you: who did it, what they did (like, comment or follow), which post and which comment, and when. We never save or show the comment's text in a notification. We don't make one for your own actions.
  • Who sees it. Only you, in the Notifications list (the bell at the top of the Feed). It shows the newest member's name and picture, how many others, the post's first photo and a short time. Members you blocked or who blocked you, and banned members, are never shown or counted.
  • When it goes. A notification is deleted when the like, comment or follow is removed (an unlike, a deleted comment or post, an unfollow, a block, or either account being deleted). Otherwise a daily job deletes notifications older than 90 days.
  • Seen. We save the time you last opened the list, to mark what is new.
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications on your phone, we also send a push notification: "<name> liked your post", "<name> and 4 others commented on your post", "<name> started following you", in your app language. It contains the member's display name (cut at 40 characters) and what they did, never the comment's text. Tapping it opens the post or the member's profile. To avoid floods there is at most one like notification per post every 30 minutes, one comment notification per post every 5 minutes, one follower notification every 30 minutes and 10 of these an hour. Nothing is sent from or to blocked or banned members. On Android phones the notifications come in two categories, "Messages" and "Likes, comments and followers", which you can turn off separately in your phone's settings.
  • Your choices. Settings → Notifications has a switch each for likes, comments and new followers (all on until you change them) and the message switch. The switches stop push notifications only; the list in the app always shows everything. You can also turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Push records. To keep the limits above, we keep a short record of which notifications were handled for a push, whether one was sent, and when (no names, no text, no token). It is removed after about 1 day (see section 7).

3.9 Search

  • Members can search for other members by display name, for shops by name and city, and for posts by the words in their captions.
  • Who can find you. Any member who can see you (no block either way, neither of you banned) can find you by name. Results show only what your profile already shows: your display name, profile picture, city and country. Being in Se duyên is never searchable and never shown in search.
  • "Show me in search". A switch in Settings, on by default. When it is off, you don't appear in member search. Your posts can still be found by their words, and you still appear everywhere else (your posts, comments and messages).
  • What we keep. No search history, on your phone or on our servers: what you type and the results are kept only on the Search screen and are gone when you leave it. To stop bulk copying, the server allows 30 searches a minute and 500 a day per member; for this it keeps, per member, only when the current minute and day of searching started and how many searches each had. That record is deleted with your account. Your search words are sent in the body of the request, not in the web address; our provider Supabase's own platform logs record the request path (for example /rpc/search_members), not the search words.

3.10 Two-step login (optional)

  • You can turn on two-step login in Settings → Two-step login. Every new login then also needs a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator), before anything else in the app. We don't use text messages.
  • The authenticator key. When you set it up, our login provider (Supabase Auth) makes a secret key and shows it to you as a QR code, an "Open authenticator app" link and a key you can copy (if you tap Copy key, it stays on your phone's clipboard like anything else you copy). Supabase Auth keeps the key with your account to check your codes. Our own code never stores it, and members can't see it. It is deleted when you turn two-step login off, when Lails support resets it, or when you delete your account. There are no backup codes.
  • Lost your authenticator? Write to lailssupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Before turning off two-step login for you, the owner checks that the request came from that address, replies to it and waits for your answer, and compares your name and profile picture. To do this the owner looks up the account by that email address in an owner-only tool, which shows the member's name, profile picture and city; the email address isn't saved. The reset removes your authenticators and signs out all your phones (our server functions refuse the old login at once; the rest of the app within about one hour).
  • Record of resets. Each reset is recorded: which account was reset, which owner did it, when, and how many authenticators and logins were removed. No email address, name or reason. See section 7.
  • While two-step login is on, deleting your account in the app also needs a code. If you have lost your authenticator, support must reset it first (or the owner deletes the account for you).

3.11 Appearance (dark mode)

The Appearance setting (Automatic, Light or Dark) is saved on your phone only, not on your account or our servers. It stays when you log out.

Photos kept on your phone. So that photos you have seen show at once next time, the app keeps copies of other members' photos it has shown (profile pictures, cover photos, post photos, and listing and room ad photos, in lists and on their detail screens) in the app's photo cache on your phone. Se duyên photos, your own face photos and the photos the Lails team looks at when it reviews a report are kept only while the app is running, and emptied at log out, never saved on the phone. The app's photo cache is emptied when you log out, when your account is deleted, or when another account logs in on the phone. Other members' phones keep copies of your photos in the same way until they log out.

3.12 What we don't collect

No precise GPS location. No street address. No phone number. No contacts. No microphone access. The camera is used only to scan QR codes (My QR code > Scan a code), and only if you allow it: no photo or video is taken, kept or sent; only the text in a Lails code is used to find the member. Profile pictures and all other photos come only from your gallery. No advertising id. No analytics or tracking tools.

My QR code. Each member has a QR code with a random code (not your account id) that opens your profile for members who could see it anyway. It is stored with your account, replaced when you tap "Make a new code" (the old code stops working), and deleted with your account. On Android, reading QR codes uses Google ML Kit on your phone, which may send Google usage diagnostics (never the camera image).

4. Sensitive information

Some data can be "sensitive" under privacy laws:

  • Photos of your face. We don't use them for face recognition (see 3.2). Some laws (for example in Illinois, Texas, Washington and the EU) have special rules for face data.
  • Se duyên "I am" and "Show me". Together they can reveal your gender identity and sexual orientation. We ask for them only if you join Se duyên, with a separate consent screen. "I am" shows on your Se duyên profile. "Show me" is never shown to anyone; it is used only to decide which singles see each other. Leaving Se duyên or deleting your account removes them, with your Se duyên photos and your Se duyên consent record.
  • Se duyên photos are shown only to other Se duyên members, and to the owner only while your Se duyên profile has an open report.

5. How we use your data

  • To run the app: your account, profile, posts, messages, listings and ads.
  • To show distances without showing where you live.
  • To keep members safe: the 18+ check, reports, blocks, bans and stopping banned people from signing up again.
  • To send you account emails (verification, password reset).
  • To send you message notifications that say "New message from <name>", never the message text. The app asks the first time you open Tám, and you can turn them off in Settings → Notifications ("Message notifications") or in your phone's settings. Messages from members you blocked or who blocked you, and from banned members, send none.
  • To show your Notifications list and send like, comment and new follower notifications (see 3.8).
  • To let members find each other, shops and posts in Search, and to limit how much one member can search (see 3.9).
  • To protect your account with two-step login if you turn it on, and to reset it when you ask support (see 3.10).
  • To keep your Saved list and show your cover photo and the pay on your job ads (see 3.2 and 3.6).
  • To answer you when you write to us (see 6, "Our support mailbox").
  • To translate a post or comment when you tap "Translate". Only then are its text and your app language sent to Google; the result is shown with "Translated by Google". If another member taps Translate on your post or comment, its text is sent to Google in the same way.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't make automatic decisions about you that have legal effects.

6. Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that run Lails:

  • Supabase stores everything: database, files, logins, server functions and live messages. Its servers for Lails are in the United States (Amazon Web Services, region us-east-1, N. Virginia). Supabase and its providers (for example Cloudflare) keep their own request logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Expo builds the app and delivers app updates. Each time the app opens, it asks Expo's servers for updates. Expo then sees your IP address, your phone's platform (for example iOS), the app version and update channel, a random install id made by the update tool (not linked to your Lails account), and, if the app crashed last time, the error message. Expo gets no account details or content.
  • Apple and Google, if you sign in with them, and Apple for the App Store.
  • Our email sender (today Supabase's built-in sender; later our own email provider) gets your email address to send account emails.
  • Google Cloud Translation gets the text of a post or comment and your app language, only when you tap "Translate" (no name, id or account). The result is shown with "Translated by Google".
  • Expo's push service and Apple's push service get a token for your phone and the notification text: "New message from <name>", or for likes, comments and new followers "<name> liked your post" and the like, with the member's display name and a count of others. They also get the kind of notification and the id of the post or member it opens (no comment or message text). Expo passes the notification to Apple on an iPhone, and to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on an Android phone.
  • Google (ML Kit) may get usage diagnostics (for example device and app information, the event type, the image format and size, error codes and a per-installation id), never the image or the face results from the face check on your phone (see 3.2).
  • Our support mailbox. If you write to lailssupport@gmail.com (also through Thêm > "Góp ý cho Lails" / Send feedback, which opens your own email app), we receive your email address, your name as your email app shows it and what you write, in our Gmail mailbox (Google). We use it only to answer you and to improve Lails.

Other members see what is described in section 3. We may also share data if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or if Lails is sold or reorganized (we would tell you first).

7. How long we keep it

  • Most data: as long as your account exists, or until you delete it (a post, a listing, an ad).
  • Cover photo: until you change or remove it, or delete your account. A cover an owner removed stays in your private folder (only you can see it) until you delete your account.
  • Saved items: until you unsave the item, the item is deleted, or you delete your account.
  • Expired listings and ads are hidden after 30 days. Chợ chồm hổm listings and their photos, Mướn phòng room ads and their photos, and Tìm ông bà nội job ads are deleted 90 days after they expire if you don't renew them. This daily clean-up is built and will be switched on before launch.
  • Unfinished sign-ups: Google and Apple sign-ups that stop before the date of birth step are deleted after 24 hours. Email sign-ups never verified are deleted after 7 days without activity; verified but never finished, after 30 days without activity. This email clean-up is built and will be switched on before launch.
  • Notifications list: a notification goes when its like, comment or follow goes; otherwise a daily job deletes it once it is more than 90 days old.
  • Push records: which messages, likes, comments and follows were handled for a push notification, and whether one was sent (no names, no text, no token), are removed after about 1 day.
  • Search limit counters: one small record per member (when the current minute and day of searching started, and the counts), until the account is deleted. No search words.
  • Two-step login: the authenticator key is kept by Supabase Auth until you turn two-step login off, support resets it, or you delete your account.
  • Owner action records: the record of each two-step login reset (which account, which owner, when, how many authenticators and logins were removed) and the record of each account the owner deleted (which account, which owner, when) are kept with no end date, also after the accounts are deleted. They hold account ids but no email, name or reason.
  • Reports are kept until the reported item or one of the accounts is deleted, also after a report is dismissed.
  • Banned accounts: if a banned account is deleted, we keep a scrambled code (a keyed one-way hash) of its email and Google or Apple account, with no link to any other data, so the same email or account can't sign up again. Today this is kept with no end date. Because we hold the key, we can check whether a given email is on the list.
  • Under-18 Google / Apple block: if someone under 18 tries to sign up with Google or Apple, we delete the account at once and keep only a scrambled code of the Google or Apple account and the date they turn 18. A daily job deletes it on that date.
  • Login records: our login provider's audit log keeps your email address and IP address for sign-ups and logins, and login sessions keep the IP address and device type, and it is not deleted when you delete your account today.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in our provider's backups for up to 7 days (daily backups on the planned Supabase Pro plan).
  • Request logs: our provider's request logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for 7 days (planned Supabase Pro plan).

8. Your rights and choices

  • See and change your data. Your profile, area, picture, posts, listings and ads are all in the app, and you can change them there. To ask for a copy of your data, write to lailssupport@gmail.com.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile tab → Settings → Delete account. "Delete account" is also on the sign-up screens, so you can leave before you finish. You confirm with a fresh login and tap "Delete my account permanently": an email account enters its password; a Google or Apple account signs in with Google or Apple again. On an Android phone, signing in with Google again is followed by your phone's own lock (fingerprint, face unlock or the phone PIN, pattern or password), if one is set; your phone does that check and Lails only learns whether it passed (see 3.3). If two-step login is on, you also enter your 6-digit code. This deletes your account and all of this: your profile, profile picture, cover photo (and any old cover files), Se duyên profile and photos, posts and their photos and videos, comments, likes, follows, blocks, reports, listings and their photos, room ads, job ads, the shop reviews you wrote, your notification tokens and notification settings, your notifications (and the ones your likes, comments and follows made for others), your push records, your search limit counter, your saved items (and other members' saves of your ads), your My QR code, your two-step login authenticators and the messages you sent. The person you chatted with keeps their own messages. Shops you added stay (without your name). For Apple accounts we also ask Apple to remove Lails' access. A suspended (banned) member can still delete their account from the suspension screen. See also How to delete your Lails account.
  • Delete your account without the app: email lailssupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.
  • Location. You can say no to phone location and type your city instead. You can change your area in Settings.
  • Optional features. Face ID or fingerprint unlock, two-step login, message notifications, like, comment and new follower notifications, "Show me in search" and Se duyên are optional and can be turned off.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to correct data, object, restrict use, move your data, withdraw consent, and complain to a privacy regulator. Write to lailssupport@gmail.com. We answer within the time the law where you live requires.

9. Security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Photos and videos are in private storage, shown only through short-lived links.
  • Strict database rules decide who can read each record. Members can't read other members' private fields, even with technical tools. Some tables (for example posts and post photos, shop reviews, likes and notification tokens) can't be read directly by members at all; the app gets what it may show only through server functions that apply blocks and bans.
  • Your login on the phone is stored in the phone's secure storage.
  • Optional two-step login: a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on every new login (see 3.10). Owners must use it for the owner tools.
  • Deleting an account needs a fresh login (within 5 minutes).
  • No system is perfectly safe. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
  • Tám messages are not end-to-end encrypted (see 3.6).

10. International users

Lails is used worldwide, mainly in the United States and Vietnam. Your data is stored in the United States (Amazon Web Services us-east-1, N. Virginia, through Supabase). If you use Lails outside the United States, your data is sent there.

11. Children
